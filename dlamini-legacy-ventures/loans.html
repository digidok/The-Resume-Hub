Our Product

Short-Term Loans

A simple, once-off short-term loan of up to R2,000 to help with unexpected expenses — assessed responsibly, every time.

What is a short-term loan?

A short-term loan is a once-off amount, up to R2,000, that is repaid — together with interest and applicable fees — on an agreed repayment date. It's designed to help cover a short-term cash-flow gap, not ongoing borrowing.

Who can apply?

South African consumers who meet our lending criteria, including a minimum affordability threshold, valid identification and a regular source of income. All applications remain subject to verification and approval.

Loan features

  • Loan amounts from R500 up to R2,000
  • Clear, itemised cost of credit before you sign
  • Online application from any smartphone or computer
  • Affordability assessment on every application
  • Written credit agreement with full disclosure of terms
  • Support available via phone, email and WhatsApp
Not everyone will qualify. Loans are granted subject to verification, affordability assessment, credit assessment and approval — approval is never guaranteed and is not based on convenience or urgency alone.
