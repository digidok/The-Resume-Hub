Our Process

How it works

From application to decision, here's exactly what to expect.

1

Apply Online

Complete our secure online application form with your personal, employment and financial details. It takes about 10 minutes on your phone or computer.

2

Verification

We verify the information you've provided, including your identity, income and supporting documents.

3

Assessment

Your application is assessed based on affordability and our applicable lending criteria — this determines whether a loan is appropriate for your circumstances.

4

Decision & Disbursement

If approved, you'll receive your loan agreement setting out the amount, cost and repayment terms. After you accept and any required steps are completed, funds are disbursed according to the agreed terms.

Every application is treated individually. We do not promise a specific approval time or outcome — assessment only begins once we have the information and verification we need.
