Responsible Lending

Responsible lending starts with affordability. Here's what that means in practice.

Affordability assessment

Before any loan is approved, we assess whether the loan is affordable for you — taking into account your income, existing debt obligations and regular expenses. A loan will not be granted where it appears you cannot afford to repay it.

We will never

  • Guarantee approval before an assessment has taken place
  • Encourage you to borrow more than you need
  • Approve a loan we believe you cannot afford to repay
  • Pressure you into accepting a loan agreement

If you're struggling to repay

Contact us as soon as possible if you think you may struggle to repay on time. We would rather work with you than let a missed payment escalate. See our FAQs and Complaints pages for more information, or consider speaking to a registered debt counsellor.

Our full affordability assessment methodology, pricing structure and credit agreement wording are subject to review and approval by management and a legal/compliance professional before this product is offered to real customers.
