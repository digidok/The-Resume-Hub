Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow?

You can apply for up to R2,000, subject to our lending criteria and affordability assessment.

How quickly will I receive a decision?

Applications are assessed once the required information and verification have been completed. We don't promise a specific approval time, as this depends on how quickly we can verify your details.

Will everyone qualify?

No. All applications are subject to the applicable assessment and approval criteria, and approval is never guaranteed.

What documents do I need?

The documents required will depend on your application and verification requirements, but typically include your South African ID, proof of income, proof of address and a recent bank statement.

How do I repay my loan?

Your applicable repayment method and repayment date will be specified in your credit agreement.

What happens if I cannot repay on time?

Contact Dlamini Legacy Ventures as soon as possible. You'll be provided with the applicable information concerning your rights and obligations — see our Complaints page for how to reach us.
