Application Status

Once submitted, your application will move through one of the following statuses. We never tell you that you're approved until the actual approval process has been completed.

Application Received  Your application has been received and is queued for assessment.

Verification Required  We need to verify one or more details you've provided.

Under Assessment  Your application is being assessed against our affordability and lending criteria.

Additional Information Required  We need more information or documents from you.

Approved  Your application has been approved — your loan agreement will follow.

Declined  Your application was not approved on this occasion.

Cancelled  The application was cancelled by you or by us.
