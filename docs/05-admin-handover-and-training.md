# 5. Administrator handover and training guide

Sign in with an admin account, then open **Business hub** in the sidebar. The top bar of the hub has: Requests, Enquiries, Screening, Tasks, Retainers, Clients, Pricing, Reports, Privacy, Audit log.

## 5.1 Daily routine

1. **Enquiries**: work through new ones. Set status to Contacted, then Quoted, then Won or Lost. The "Career" and "Business" filters separate the two funnels.
2. **Requests**: new Document Studio requests. Open one to see the brief and uploads, change status, upload finished files, create quotes and invoices, and reply to the client in Messages.
3. **Tasks**: allocate work to staff with a due date, link it to a request, and move it through To do, In progress, Done. Overdue dates show in red.
4. **Reports**: glance at revenue, outstanding invoices, conversion and overdue tasks.

## 5.2 From enquiry to paid work

1. An enquiry arrives (the client gets an automatic acknowledgement).
2. Contact the client and agree scope.
3. Open or create the client's request and **create a quote** (draft). Check it, then **Send to client**. They receive an email and see it in their dashboard.
4. When they **accept**, you are emailed. Do the work and upload files to **Delivered files**. Set the request to **Delivered**; the client is emailed.
5. The client can approve the delivery and leave a rating.
6. **Create an invoice** and **Send to client**. The client can pay online with Payfast or by EFT. Online payments mark themselves **Paid** once Payfast confirms. For EFT, press **Mark paid** when the money arrives.

Clients only see what is linked to the email they sign in with, and only once that email is verified. Create quotes and retainers using the exact email the client uses.

## 5.3 Retainers and corporate contracts

Retainers page: create a plan (company, client email, hours per month, fee; choose Monthly retainer or Corporate contract and an optional end date). Log time as it is worked. The client sees a usage bar for the current month.

## 5.4 Background screening (standard procedure)

1. Confirm the lawful purpose with the employer and write it in **New screening case**. Tick only the checks the role needs.
2. Make sure the candidate has received the privacy information and given consent. Record it with **Record consent** (note how it was given). **Checks cannot be started until consent is recorded.**
3. Move each check to In progress and Completed as the provider reports. Use **Under review** for discrepancies and add a note for the client.
4. Upload the report; only the ordering client can download it.
5. A result informs a decision; it must not be the only reason to reject someone. If a candidate disputes a result, put a note on the case and re-check with the provider.
6. Never describe the business as a credit bureau or accredited provider. Only coordinate credit checks for roles where the legal conditions are met.

## 5.5 Pricing and availability

**Pricing** page: for any service, type a new starting price, tick **Request a quote** to hide the number, or untick **Visible** to hide the service. Save. Public pages update within a few minutes. **Reset** returns the service to its default in the catalogue file. Prices marked as test prices in the brief should be confirmed before launch.

## 5.6 Privacy (POPIA) requests

The **Privacy** page lists requests from the public form. Verify the person's identity before releasing or deleting anything. Set the status as you progress and keep a note of what you did. Marketing opt-outs must be honoured at once.

## 5.7 Audit log

Every sensitive business action is recorded with who did it and when. Entries cannot be edited or deleted. Check it after any dispute.

## 5.8 Draft policy wording (needs owner and legal approval before publishing)

- **Cancellation**: a client may cancel a request before work starts for a full refund of any amount paid. Once work has started, the client pays for work done to that point.
- **Refunds**: if delivered work does not match the agreed scope, we correct it at no charge; if we cannot, we refund the portion not delivered.
- **Retainers**: either side may end a monthly retainer with one month's notice; unused monthly hours do not roll over unless agreed in writing.
- **Third-party fees** (for example CIPC) are passed on at cost and are not refundable once paid to the third party.

Do not publish these until the business owner and a legal adviser have confirmed them.

## 5.9 Adding another administrator

Admins are users whose profile role is `admin` (Users page in the admin area). Only grant it to staff who need access to client documents and screening reports, because admins can see all client data.
